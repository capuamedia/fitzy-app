Before we fuel

Please read and accept our customer agreement.

1. Services provided

Fitzy delivers fuel into the vehicles you designate, at the location you provide, during a scheduled delivery window.

2. Access to your vehicle

You agree that fuel ports will be clearly accessible and unlocked during your delivery window. If we cannot reach a fuel port, we may be unable to complete the delivery.

3. Your vehicle and its condition

You confirm the vehicles you register are in a safe condition to receive fuel and are parked somewhere a delivery vehicle can safely reach.

4. Where we will and will not park

Our driver will use reasonable judgement about where to position the delivery vehicle and may decline a location that cannot be served safely.

5. Fuel type

You are responsible for confirming the correct fuel grade for each vehicle. Please check the grade shown for each vehicle when you book.

6. Payment

Delivery fees and tips are charged when you book. Fuel is charged after delivery, based on the gallons actually delivered, at the rate posted for your delivery day.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel without charge up to two hours before the start of your delivery window. Cancellations inside that window may be charged a fee.

8. Photographs

We photograph the pump meter at each fill as a record of the gallons delivered. These photographs are kept with your invoice.
